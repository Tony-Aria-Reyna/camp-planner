# NZ Camp Planner

**Open the app: https://tonyandreynanz-max.github.io/camp-planner/**

Find New Zealand campsites, plan walks the kids can manage, and turn it all into a trip plan you can print or send.

- 1,758 campsites from DOC, Auckland Council regional parks and OpenStreetMap, with prices, facilities and filters
- Day-by-day trip plans with drive times, campsites, walks, checklists, meals and a safety section
- Walk routes that follow tracks, with climbs, a height chart and walking times for adults and children
- Live progress on the trail, walk recording, and Garmin GPX import/export
- Works offline once installed: on a phone, open the link and choose **Add to Home Screen**

Everything you enter stays on your own device. See `README.txt` for how the site is published.
