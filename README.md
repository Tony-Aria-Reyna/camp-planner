# NZ Camp Planner

**Open the app: https://tonyandreynanz-max.github.io/camp-planner/**

Find New Zealand campsites, plan walks the kids can manage, and turn it all into a trip plan you can print or send. On a phone, open the link and choose **Add to Home Screen** — it then works like an app, including offline for map areas you have viewed.

## What it does

**Find campsites**
- 1,758 campsites from DOC, Auckland Council regional parks and OpenStreetMap
- Filter by price, facilities (toilets, showers, kitchen, power…), setting (beach, lake, river, bush), tents, dogs, parking and distance; ◎ Near me
- Tick sites ✓ Been, ★ Favourite or ⚑ To try; add your own sites

**Plan walks** (5 · Routes)
- Draw a route that follows walking tracks; distance, climb and drop, height chart, slope colours on the map
- Walking times and grades for adults, teens, children (8–12) and young children (5–7)
- GPX import and export for Garmin and other GPS devices

**Plan the trip** (3 · Trips)
- Day by day: where you sleep, car park / meeting point, drive times by road, walks for each day
- Type of camping (car boot, tent, walk-in, campervan, day trip) with matching checklists
- Meal plan and food lists that scale to the group (children at full, ¾ or half portions); your own recipes
- Safety and intentions: who is going, trusted contact, overdue time, pre-trip checks
- Trip sheet to print, save as PDF or send — with map, directions, website and phone links throughout
- Share a single trip with someone by code

**On the trail**
- 📍 Live: where you are, km done and to go, climb left, time left and when you will be back
- Record your walk — works with the phone locked (gaps are filled along your planned route)
- 4 · Journal: log each stay with photos and watch your map fill up

## Privacy

Everything you enter stays on your own device. Nobody can see anyone else's trips. To move your plans between devices, use **6 · Data → transfer code**.

## Data

Campsites: Crown Copyright, Department of Conservation Te Papa Atawhai (CC BY 4.0); Auckland Council website; © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Maps: OpenTopoMap, Esri, OpenStreetMap. Heights: Copernicus via Open-Meteo. Routing: FOSSGIS / OpenStreetMap.

Times, grades and campsite details are a guide — check DOC or council alerts and the forecast before you go. Emergency: 111.
